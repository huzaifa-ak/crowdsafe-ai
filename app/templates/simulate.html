What to simulate

Leaving empties a full venue through its exits. Arriving fills an empty one through its entrances, with people walking to a place and staying there.

Crowd

At one or two people per m² there is very little room to pass in a seating area, so an arrival can jam before everyone is in. That is a real effect rather than a glitch, and the run says so when it happens.

How many of them are VIPs, counted out of the total above. VIPs have places in the restricted area and reach it through a checkpoint, one person per lane cell per tick. Everybody else stays in the general area and never enters the lane.

The restricted area is whatever part of the plan can only be reached by passing a checkpoint, so painting a checkpoint across the way in is all it takes to create one.

Behaviour

Nearest is an unsignposted crowd heading for the closest door. Managed assumes marshals or clear signage spreading people between doors.
